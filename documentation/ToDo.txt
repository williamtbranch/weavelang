### Primary Objectives & Guiding Principles

- **Goal:** Implement a scalable, cost-effective data pipeline using a "Common Pool"
  for reusable, pre-processed linguistic data.
- **Architecture:** A two-part system. Part 1 is the "Pool Manager" which generates
  and caches reusable, language-specific data. Part 2 is the "Pipeline Runner"
  which consumes pooled data to generate pair-specific artifacts.
- **Golden Rule:** Expensive, reusable operations are done ONCE and cached. Data integrity
  between the Python producer and Rust consumer is paramount.
