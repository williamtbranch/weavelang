S3: by Franz Kafka
S4: Chapter I
S5: One morning, when Gregor Samsa woke from troubled dreams, he found himself transformed in his bed into a horrible vermin.
S6: He lay on his armour-like back, and if he lifted his head a little he could see his brown belly, slightly domed and divided by arches into stiff sections.
S7: The bedding was hardly able to cover it and seemed ready to slide off any moment.
S8: His many legs, pitifully thin compared with the size of the rest of him, waved about helplessly as he looked.
S9: “What’s happened to me?” he thought.
S10: It wasn’t a dream.
S11: His room, a proper human room although a little too small, lay peacefully between its four familiar walls.
S12: A collection of textile samples lay spread out on the table — Samsa was a travelling salesman — and above it there hung a picture that he had recently cut out of an illustrated magazine and housed in a nice, gilded frame.
