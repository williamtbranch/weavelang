Metamorfosis

por Franz Kafka

Chapter I

Una morning, when Gregor Samsa se despertó de bad dreams, he saw que he se había convertido en his bed en a very bad kind of bug.

He lay on his back, which was hard like a shell, and if he moved his head up a little, he could see his brown stomach, which was a little round and had lines that made it into hard parts.

The covers on la bed no podían really cover him, y it looked like they se caerían soon.

He had many legs que eran very thin, y this era sad de ver because el resto de his body era so big. When he looked a them, they se movían but he no podía control las.

“Qué ha pasado to me?” he thought.

No era un dream.

His room era una normal room for una person, but a little small, y era quiet between las four walls he knew well.

Pieces de cloth for his work estaban on la table, as he era un man que traveled to sell things. Above la table, una picture he had recently cut de una magazine estaba en la wall en un nice, gold-colored frame.
